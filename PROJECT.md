# Extroverts — Project Overview & Documentation

> **"Strangers. Hangouts. Memories."**  
> Extroverts is a hyper-local social experience and hangout platform designed to connect people through real-world meetups, curated parties, themed activities, and shared vibes.

---

## 1. What the App Does

**Extroverts** breaks the barrier of digital isolation by empowering users to host, discover, and join real-life social gatherings in their city. Rather than passive swiping, the platform focuses on active, intent-driven meetups centered around specific interests, themes, and trusted community vibes.

```
Discover / Host Meetup ──► Match Vibes & Privacy ──► Earn Vibe Tokens ──► Real-Life Memories
```

---

## 2. Core Pillars & Value Proposition

- **Real-World Connection First**: Designed to turn online strangers into real-world crews and lasting memories.
- **Vibe-Driven Matching**: Hangouts are categorized by authentic social moods, party themes, and curated activities.
- **Safety & Exclusivity**: Robust privacy controls including private circles and gender-exclusive hangouts.
- **Social Gamification**: Reputation and loyalty system driven by **Honorary Vibe Tokens (HVTs)** and tiered club memberships.

---

## 3. Key App Features

### 🌟 1. Real-Time Hangout & Party Hosting

- **Endless Excuses to Party**: Users can spontaneously create hangouts for any occasion (e.g., _"Because snacks exist"_, _"Game Night"_, _"Promotion"_, _"Unwinding"_, _"Waffle Cravings"_).
- **Curated Party Themes**:
  - ☕ **Tea Party** & 🥞 **Brunch Outing**
  - 🍽️ **Dinner Events** & 🧺 **Picnic Days**
  - 🎸 **Music Jam** & 🎤 **Karaoke Night**
  - 📚 **Book Club** & 🎲 **Board Games**
  - 🎬 **Movie Squad** & 🎮 **Game Night**
  - 🤝 **Networking** & 💪 **Workout Sessions**
  - ⚽ **Sports FC** & ⛰️ **City Hikes**

### 👥 2. Capacity & Intimacy Customization

Hosts have granular control over group sizes depending on the event style:

- **3 Guests**: Intimate gatherings
- **5 Guests**: Cozy dinner parties
- **9 Guests**: Mini celebrations
- **12 Guests**: Pocket soirees
- **18 Guests**: Big nightouts
- **25+ Guests**: Midsize assemblies & open mixers

### 🔒 3. Privacy & Exclusivity Tiers

Safety and comfort are core to every hangout:

- **Confidential Gatherings**: Location and attendee list hidden until RSVP approval.
- **Girls Exclusive**: Verified women-only spaces and safe meetup circles.
- **Private Hangouts**: Invite-only and circle-specific meetups.

### 🏆 4. Gamification & Vibe Economy

- **Honorary Vibe Tokens (HVTs)**: Social currency earned by hosting positive hangouts, attending meetups, and being an engaged community member.
- **Club Tier Progression**:
  - 🥉 **Bronze Club Member**
  - 🤍 **Ivory Club Member**
  - 👑 **VIP Tier Status**
- **Awarded Superlatives & Badges**: Community-awarded titles that highlight each user's unique vibe (e.g., _"Best Playlist"_, _"Chief Energizer"_, _"Top Conversationalist"_).

### 💬 5. Chat, Share & Relive

- **Live Event Group Chats**: Coordinate meetup spots, timings, and logistics in real-time.
- **Shared Memory Feed**: Upload photos, recap moments, and stay connected with people you met.
- **City Feeds**: Browse trending nearby events (e.g., _"Waffle cravings any1?"_, _"The Hunger Games @ Barbeque Nation"_, _"Hikers in the city?"_).

---

## 4. Target Audience

- **Young Professionals & Students**: Looking to expand their local social network in new or current cities.
- **Extroverts & Social Creators**: People who love hosting dinners, parties, game nights, and jamming sessions.
- **Hobbyists & Activity Seekers**: Individuals searching for partners for workouts, sports, book clubs, movies, or cafe hopping.
- **Communities & Circles**: Groups wanting a structured, safe tool to organize regular private gatherings.
